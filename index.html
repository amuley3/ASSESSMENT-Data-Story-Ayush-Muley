Phoenix by the numbers

Phoenix is one of the biggest cities in the United States, with more than 1.6 million people living there. But the size of Phoenix is about more than just its population. The city also has thousands of miles of streets, hundreds of public schools, large desert parks, and miles of trails. Looking at these numbers gives us a better idea of how much the city has to manage. This data story looks at some of the numbers behind Phoenix and asks: How large is Phoenix when we look beyond its population?

D1 · Story Frame

Phoenix is a large city with more than 1.6 million people. It also has thousands of miles of streets, hundreds of public schools, and large areas of desert parks and mountains. These numbers help show that Phoenix is more than just its population. In this story, I will look at these numbers to better understand how large Phoenix is and what the city has to manage. I used data from the City of Phoenix Facts and Statistics webpage. The numbers were taken directly from the city’s website and were not changed.
